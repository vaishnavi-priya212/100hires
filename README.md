## Research Project: AI-Powered SEO Content Production

### Topic
AI-Powered SEO Content Production — researching how leading practitioners create, optimize, and distribute content for AI-driven search (Google AI Overviews, LLM-based answer engines, GEO/AEO/LLMO).

B2B SaaS companies increasingly need content strategies built for AI-mediated discovery, not just traditional rankings. This research collects firsthand practitioner insight to support a future playbook on AI-powered SEO content production.
