# 23 Factors That Actually Get Content Cited by AI Search Engines

**Author:** Cyrus Shepard
**Date posted:** 2026-05-07
**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/cyrusshepard/

## Content
Cyrus Shepard published an analysis reviewing 54 experiments, patents, and case studies to score 23 factors associated with AI citation (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity). The highest-scoring signals were URL accessibility, traditional search rank, "fan-out" query coverage, preview/indexing control, and direct query-answer match. He explicitly caveated that these are correlations from evidence, not a confirmed ranking formula from any platform.

## Why it's useful
Provides a prioritized, evidence-based checklist for a content production playbook: ensure pages are crawlable/indexable, maintain strong traditional search rankings (these still feed AI citation pools), cover related query variations, and write self-contained passages that directly answer likely questions.
