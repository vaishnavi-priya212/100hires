# 70% of Brand Mentions in AI Chats Are "Dark" (Untrackable)

**Author:** Ross Simmonds
**Date posted:** 2026-04 (April 2026)
**Link:** https://ca.linkedin.com/in/rosssimmonds

## Content
Ross Simmonds shared a 2026 statistic that roughly 70% of brand mentions happening inside AI chat conversations (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) are "dark" — meaning they don't show up in analytics at all, since there's no referral traffic or trackable click. He also noted a content-format distinction: for engines like Perplexity and Gemini, content with detailed facts and numbers performs better at earning a click-through, since those engines are more likely to surface a link.

## Why it's useful
Highlights a measurement gap critical for an AI content playbook: brands may be getting significant AI-driven exposure that's invisible in traditional analytics. Also reinforces that fact-dense, numbers-heavy content performs better for engines that do link out (Perplexity/Gemini) versus engines that summarize without links.
