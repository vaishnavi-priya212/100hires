# AI Mode vs AI Overviews: How User Behavior Differs

**Author:** Kevin Indig
**Date posted:** 2026-05 (late May 2026)
**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/kevinindig/

## Content
Kevin Indig analyzed a clickstream study of 846,000 US Google sessions from February-March 2026 comparing user behavior in AI Mode versus AI Overviews. In AI Mode, users largely accept what they're given (88% take the AI's shortlist, 64% click nothing). In AI Overviews on regular search pages, the same users instead compare options more actively — cursor coverage of the page jumps significantly and back-scrolling increases. His conclusion: AI Mode optimization is a visibility problem at the model layer, while AI Overview optimization is a comparison problem on the SERP itself.

## Why it's useful
Critical distinction for a content playbook — different AI surfaces require different optimization approaches. Content for AI Mode needs to focus on being the cited/recommended source; content competing inside an AI Overview-enabled SERP still needs to win the comparison (strong titles/meta descriptions matter more than ever).
