# Critique of Google's AI Search Optimization Guide

**Author:** Mike King
**Date posted:** 2026-05-18
**Link:** https://ipullrank.com (LinkedIn discussion same day; original analysis on ipullrank.com)

## Content
Following Google's May 15, 2026 update to its official guide on optimizing for AI Overviews and AI Mode, Mike King argued that the guidance reflects Google's platform interests rather than how AI retrieval systems actually work in practice. He pushed back on Google's dismissal of GEO, content chunking, and multi-platform optimization strategies, framing the guide as a way to discourage practitioners from building expertise on platforms Google doesn't control (ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.).

## Why it's useful
Shows the gap between official platform guidance and practitioner-level reality — useful for a playbook section on "what platforms tell you vs. what actually works" for AI content visibility. Also signals that content strategies built around chunking/passage-level optimization remain relevant despite official downplaying.
