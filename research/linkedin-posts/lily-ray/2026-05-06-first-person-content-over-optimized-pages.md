# Google Favors Authentic First-Person Content Over Generic Optimized Pages

**Author:** Lily Ray
**Date posted:** 2026-05-06
**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/lily-ray-44755615/

## Content
Lily Ray noted that Google's search results have shifted to favor authentic, first-person content and forum discussions over generic, heavily-optimized pages. This follows her broader warning (in a podcast the following week) that popular GEO tactics — self-promotional listicles, scaled comparison pages, and prompt-injection via "summarize with AI" buttons — are increasingly being treated as spam and risk long-term penalties.

## Why it's useful
Critical guardrail for an AI content playbook: prioritize genuine first-person experience and authentic voice over templated "AI search optimized" formats. Tactics designed purely to game AI citations carry real penalty risk and shouldn't be the foundation of a content strategy.
