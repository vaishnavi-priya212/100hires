# May 2026 Broad Core Update: AI Overview Impact and "Mt. AI" Pattern

**Author:** Glenn Gabe
**Date posted:** 2026-05-25
**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/glenngabe/

## Content
Glenn Gabe tracked the May 2026 Google broad core update (started May 21, completed June 2), noting significant volatility especially in YMYL categories like gambling. He highlighted a pattern he calls "Mt. AI" — sites that scaled heavily with lower-quality AI-generated content saw initial traffic surges followed by sharp drops once a core update hit, whether via manual action or algorithmic detection.

## Why it's useful
Cautionary case study for an AI content production playbook: scaling AI-generated content for quick wins is a short-term strategy that core updates specifically target. Sustainable AI-assisted content needs quality oversight, not just volume.
