# 2026 Marketing Predictions: AI as Infrastructure, Not Disruptor

**Author:** Eli Schwartz
**Date posted:** 2025-12-08
**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/schwartze/

## Content
Eli Schwartz framed 2026 as the year AI shifts from being a disruptive force in marketing to simply being part of normal operations — embedded into workflows rather than a novelty or threat. He opened the discussion to his network asking what under-the-radar shifts people expect, signaling his interest in second-order effects beyond the obvious "AI changes search" narrative.

## Why it's useful
Useful framing for a playbook's tone: positions AI tooling as table-stakes infrastructure for content production rather than a special "AI content" category — content teams should plan for AI-assisted workflows as the default, not an add-on.
