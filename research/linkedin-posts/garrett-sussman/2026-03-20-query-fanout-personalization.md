# Query Fan-Out and Hyper-Personalization in AI Search

**Author:** Garrett Sussman
**Date posted:** 2026-03-20
**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/garrettsussman/

## Content
Garrett Sussman highlighted how AI search personalization is shifting "upstream" — as queries become longer and more conversational, the fan-out of related queries becomes increasingly tailored to individual user context, memory, and intent. He connected this to iPullRank's broader research on how this changes content visibility, noting his team has mapped these dynamics into an omnichannel content strategy approach.

## Why it's useful
Signals that AI content strategy can't target a single "average" query anymore — content needs to anticipate a range of fan-out query variations and personas, since AI systems generate different sub-queries depending on user context and history.
