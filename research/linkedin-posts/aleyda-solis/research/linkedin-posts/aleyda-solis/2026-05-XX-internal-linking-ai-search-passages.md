# Internal Linking and Passage-Level Relevance for AI Search

**Author:** Aleyda Solis
**Date posted:** 2026-05 (referenced in May 2026 coverage)
**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/aleyda/

## Content
Aleyda Solis emphasized that AI search systems (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) build answers by pulling specific passages or chunks from pages rather than evaluating pages as a whole. This means a page can rank well overall but still fail to get cited if the specific section answering a question is structurally weak. She frames this as a shift in how content teams should think about internal linking — it signals topic ownership and section-level relationships to AI systems, not just overall page authority.

## Why it's useful
Reinforces a core principle for an AI content playbook: structure content so each section/passage can stand alone as a complete answer, and use internal linking to signal which page "owns" a topic at the passage level — not just page-level optimization.
