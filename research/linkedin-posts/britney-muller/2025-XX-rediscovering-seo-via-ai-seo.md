# "AI SEO" / GEO Is Mostly Just Rediscovering SEO

**Author:** Britney Muller
**Date posted:** 2025 (late 2025)
**Link:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/britneymuller/

## Content
Britney Muller pushed back on the framing of "AI SEO" or GEO as an entirely new discipline. She clarified two distinct mechanisms: getting mentioned by an LLM without a URL (driven by brand presence in training data and online discussions) versus URLs appearing in LLM outputs (which come from underlying search engine APIs like Google or Bing). Her core argument: many "AI behavior patterns" people claim to discover are actually just search engine ranking factors already well understood in traditional SEO — fresh content ranks well in search, so it appears in AI outputs; spammy listicles rank well in search, so LLMs cite them too.

## Why it's useful
Important grounding perspective for a playbook: don't treat "AI SEO" as a separate skill set from traditional SEO fundamentals. Understanding how search engines rank content remains the foundation — AI visibility largely flows from search visibility, with brand-mention/citation building as the layer on top.
